# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential employers, clients, and collaborators viewing Akhilesh Kalme's portfolio. They want to quickly assess technical skill, design sense, and whether this developer fits their project or team.

## Product Purpose

A full-stack developer portfolio that demonstrates technical capability through its own design and interactivity. Success is a visitor who immediately perceives "this developer has both skill and taste" and reaches out for work.

## Brand Personality

Modern, Creative, Dynamic. The site should feel contemporary and forward-thinking, express creative confidence, and communicate energy through motion and depth.

## Anti-references

- Overly corporate or templated portfolio sites (generic Bootstrap themes)
- Skeleton-loading or half-baked "under construction" pages
- Gimmicky 3D that feels like a tech demo rather than a professional presentation
- Dark patterns, autoplay audio, or intrusive popups

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — The design itself proves the developer's skill. Every interaction should feel intentional and polished.
2. **Depth with purpose** — 3D and motion should serve clarity and hierarchy, not distract. Each effect should guide attention or convey meaning.
3. **Confident simplicity** — Clean layouts with bold moments. Let content breathe; use whitespace as an active design element.
4. **Professional edge** — Premium feel without being flashy. Think Apple meets Awwwards, not MySpace.
5. **Effortless performance** — Smooth 60fps animations, fast load times, accessible to all devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Support reduced motion preferences
- Sufficient color contrast ratios
- Keyboard-navigable interactive elements
- Semantic HTML structure
